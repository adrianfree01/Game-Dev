# Game Development Journey
This is my attempt (not my first) at writing a game engine

Every other time I've tried this, I get halfway through writing how to render a screen and give up.
Not this time...

The idea here is simple
 1. Write an engine
 2. Make a game
 3. Profit

Will I follow through? Will this be a giant waste of time? We will see

The purpose of this is not to make money, although that would be nice.
Since graduating University, and getting an entry-level job, I have become way too reliant on AI for everything. 
In my work, I don't mind using AI, but I am worried of losing my ability to code, design, and think.

Of course, it is Q4 2026 right now, and AI is as prominent a tool as ever, and will I use it on this project? Yes.
_BUT_ I have agents set up, and in there instructions they are told to never EVER write code, tell me what to fix and how
or even debug.

My AI environment is simply meant to mentor me through this project. Not do it for me.
